Basic Information

This portal allows students of UITS Department of CSE to check their attendance fines for the Spring 2026 semester. Enter your Student ID in the lookup section to view your detailed fine breakdown.

Fine Policy

• Total fine per student is capped at BDT 500 regardless of course-wise accumulation.

• Students with attendance below 20% in any course must contact the department before collecting admit cards.

• Approved waivers are deducted from the total fine amount.

• For queries, contact the Department of CSE office.
